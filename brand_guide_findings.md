# Citizen Relations brand guide findings

## High-level visual character

The brand system is minimal, editorial, confident, and typographically led. Large-format type dominates the layouts, often with generous margins and very sparse supporting elements. The visual language depends more on proportion, whitespace, contrast, and typographic confidence than on decoration.

## Typography direction observed

- The typography appears to use a modern, clean sans-serif with a highly polished editorial feel.
- Headlines are oversized, tightly composed, and visually dominant.
- Supporting labels and navigation use very small uppercase or small-caps-like text with generous tracking.
- The brand emphasizes strong hierarchy through scale rather than through many font weights or colors.
- Layouts use restrained typography on utility text and dramatic typography on hero statements.

## Structural layout cues

- Backgrounds are often off-white or sand rather than pure white.
- Content blocks use large quiet fields of negative space.
- Black/charcoal text on pale neutral backgrounds creates the main contrast model.
- Accent red is used sparingly and should remain an accent rather than the dominant UI color.
- Pages feel premium because they are calm, spacious, and grid-disciplined.

## Logo / motif observations

- The identity uses an interlocking, geometric wordmark system.
- The mark feels architectural and modular.
- This suggests FounderOps should borrow the sense of structure and discipline, not literally copy the logo.

## FounderOps translation direction

- Shift from the current neon-dark operator-console look to a warmer editorial workspace.
- Use sand/off-white backgrounds with charcoal text and restrained slate surfaces.
- Keep red as a precise accent for active states, key actions, or critical focus rather than widespread highlights.
- Improve typography with stronger editorial hierarchy, more breathing room, and cleaner utility labels.
- Make cards feel quieter and more print-like: thinner borders, softer shadows, flatter surfaces, and clearer spacing.
