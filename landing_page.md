## FAQ
- **Is my data private?** Yes—data stays in your workspace; we don’t train on your inputs. Exports anytime.
- **What calendars are supported?** Google Calendar now; Outlook support planned.
- **Do I have to use automated scheduling?** No—every suggestion can be edited or pinned manually.
- **How accurate are AI time estimates?** We blend your history + task type; you can nudge durations and we learn.
- **Will it flood me with news?** Signals are curated per industry with adjustable noise filters.
- **What if a meeting pops up?** We reflow blocks instantly, preserving deadlines and your protected focus windows.
